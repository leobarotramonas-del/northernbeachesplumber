# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro static site, following the established Northern Beaches Plumber project architecture. The site must remain compatible with Cloudflare static deployment, use minimal client-side JavaScript, centralised TypeScript data and Markdown content collections.

## Users

Primary users are Northern Beaches homeowners, tenants, strata contacts and property managers who need to understand a plumbing problem, assess whether the business offers the relevant service and call to discuss a booking. They may be on a phone, under time pressure and dealing with water damage, blocked fixtures, hot-water failure, gas concerns or an unexplained leak.

## Product Purpose

The website explains the available plumbing services, the assessment and repair process, suggested Northern Beaches coverage and how to contact the business. Success means visitors can quickly find accurate service information, understand what to prepare before calling and use the telephone action without misleading claims.

## Positioning

The Northern Beaches Plumber presents a licensed plumbing service through clear, problem-led guidance, transparent booking expectations and useful local property context rather than unsupported speed, price or availability promises.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive from search on mobile, navigate between services and suburb pages, and call with a full address, property type, symptoms and access details. Blog publishing is handled through Astro Markdown content files. Static deployment is prepared for Cloudflare.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Required page architecture, trailing slashes, internal links, sitemaps, robots rules and structured data are defined in the supplied build brief.
- Customer-facing language is Australian English.
- Suggested suburb coverage, postcodes and actual availability remain subject to business verification before publishing.
- The site must not invent completed jobs, local offices, response times, equipment, review scores, addresses or other business claims.
- Draft blog posts must never appear in public routes or sitemaps.
- Deployment configuration may be prepared, but publishing requires a separate user request.

## Product Principles

1. Explain the plumbing problem and next step before selling the service.
2. Make calling easy without promising unverified timing or coverage.
3. Keep local content useful and distinct while clearly marking coverage as subject to confirmation.
4. Preserve crawlable architecture, fast static delivery and accessible interaction.
5. Prefer verified business facts and transparent limits over promotional claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable navigation and controls, visible focus indicators, a skip link, descriptive labels, reduced-motion support, a pause control for video and layouts that remain usable under text zoom without content overlap.
